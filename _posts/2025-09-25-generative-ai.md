## Foundation Models
Foundation models are large-scale AI models trained on massive datasets, capable of performing a wide range of tasks (e.g., text, image, code generation). Examples include GPT-4, PaLM, LLaMA, and Stable Diffusion. [Learn more](https://crfm.stanford.edu/2021/08/26/what-are-foundation-models.html)

## Agents & Workflows
Agents are autonomous entities that can perform tasks, interact with users, or other agents, and make decisions. Workflows define sequences of steps for agents to accomplish complex tasks. [Agentic AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.01864)

## Hugging Face
[Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) is a popular platform for sharing models, datasets, and building AI workflows. It supports transformers, RAG, and many other generative AI tools.

## Datasets
Datasets are collections of data used to train and evaluate AI models. Find open datasets at [Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets), [Google Dataset Search](https://datasetsearch.research.google.com/), and [AWS Open Data](https://registry.opendata.aws/).

## Vector Databases
Vector databases store embeddings (vector representations) for efficient similarity search and retrieval in generative AI applications. Popular options: [Pinecone](https://www.pinecone.io/), [Weaviate](https://weaviate.io/), [Milvus](https://milvus.io/).
